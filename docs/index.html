    How the numbers and checks work

    FP, PWR, POP and SPN are percentiles: 80 means the paddle beats 80% of tested paddles. Power is serve speed (mph), pop is punch-volley speed (mph), and firepower combines the two. Tap a paddle to see the raw mph and rpm.

    Grit durability tiers show how long the face keeps its spin: Tier 1 is a durable texture and Tier 4 is raw peel ply, which wears fastest.

    Match score (0–100) is the average of power, pop, firepower and spin percentiles, grit durability (Tier 1 = 100, Tier 2 = 67, Tier 3 = 33, Tier 4 = 0) and price (cheaper scores higher).

    John Kew check: paddles found in the John Kew database get their power, pop and spin ranked within John Kew's own data. A check mark means each rank is within 20 points of Pickleball Effect's; "differs" names the ones that aren't. Ranks are compared, not raw RPM, because the two labs measure spin differently.
